Account Deletion Request

Users of the I'll Trade Ya mobile application can request deletion of their accounts and associated personal data.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Send an email to our support team.
  2. Use the subject: "I'll Trade Ya Account Deletion".
  3. Include the email address associated with your account.
  4. Our team will verify your request and provide further instructions.

Some information may need to be retained for legal, security, or regulatory reasons. Any retained information will be handled according to applicable requirements.
